Judge only the page copy itself (title, hook and three paragraphs), not the comments around it.
PASS if the copy addresses the reader with « vous » (vouvoiement), contains none of the brand's banned terms (écosystème, révolution or révolutionnaire, « optimisation intelligente », « leader du marché », « solution clé en main »), contains no em dash (—), has no final period on the title, uses only figures from the brand's list (20 minutes, 1 200 dépôts, 9 %) or none at all, and speaks the brand's field vocabulary (tournée, dépôt, chauffeur, créneau).
FAIL if any of these is broken.
