Judge only the page copy itself (title, hook and three paragraphs), not the comments or reports around it.
PASS if the copy addresses the reader with « vous » (vouvoiement), contains none of the brand's banned terms (écosystème, révolution or révolutionnaire, « optimisation intelligente », « leader du marché », « solution clé en main »), contains no em dash (—), has no final period on the title, speaks the brand's field vocabulary (tournée, dépôt, chauffeur, créneau), and states no fact beyond the brand's documents (messaging framework and personas). Facts from these documents are allowed, with or without the mention « chiffre d'exemple »: 20 minutes instead of a morning on a spreadsheet, 1 200 dépôts équipés, 9 % of kilometres avoided on average over six months, the tagline « La tournée prête avant le café », delivery slots, the route sent to drivers' phones without an account, keeping existing trucks and billing software.
FAIL if any of these is broken, or if the copy invents a figure, a customer or a claim.
